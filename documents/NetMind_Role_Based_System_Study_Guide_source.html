Project charter and success measures

Role objective. Study this topic to prepare for the capstone presentation. As the Project Manager, John Arvie Capulo should be ready to explain how this part of NetMind supports scope, delivery, risks, stakeholder communication, approvals, and release readiness.

System knowledge

NetMind is a volleyball analytics and team-management system. It keeps users, teams, memberships, trainer assignments, seasons, matches, drills, training assignments, performance logs, wellness check-ins, attendance, injuries, lineups, rankings, comparisons, notifications, and push subscriptions.

Database relationships and constraints protect integrity: user roles and volleyball positions are restricted, team membership is unique, check-ins are unique per player and date, attendance statuses are controlled, and records use foreign keys to connect a player, staff member, team, season, or match.
